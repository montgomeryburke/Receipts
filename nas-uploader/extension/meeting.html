How it hears Teams

Teams app (Windows): click Teams app, choose Entire screen, tick Also share system audio, then Share. Only the sound is used — the picture is thrown away straight away.

Teams in Chrome: click Teams in Chrome, pick the tab with the meeting and keep Also share tab audio on. You still hear the meeting as normal.

Your microphone is heard separately (Chrome asks once). With speakers instead of a headset, the meeting can echo into your mic; the assistant drops those duplicates, but a headset works best.

Chrome shows a "sharing" bar while it listens. Click Stop and save here (or Stop sharing in that bar) to stop.
