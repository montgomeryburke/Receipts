How Chrome asks

Chrome shows its sharing picker each time. For the computer's sound choose Entire screen and tick Also share system audio; for one tab pick the tab and keep Also share tab audio on. For the screen, pick a screen, a window or a tab. Chrome shows a "sharing" bar while it records — Stop sharing there also ends and saves the recording.

Audio-only recordings keep only the sound; the picture is thrown away straight away.

Recording other people may need their consent — follow your workplace's rules.
