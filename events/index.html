How to get the free keys

Ticketmaster (concerts, sports, motorsports, comedy):
1. Go to developer.ticketmaster.com and create a free account.
2. It creates an app for you automatically — open "My Apps" and copy the Consumer Key.
3. Paste it above. That's it — the free tier allows 5,000 searches a day.

SeatGeek (includes resale tickets, similar to StubHub):
1. Go to seatgeek.com/account/develop, sign up, register an app.
2. Copy the Client ID and paste it above.

My tracked pages (race tracks, wing nights…)

Add the public website of any race track, racing org, fair, or restaurant. Event Scout scans each page for dates in the next 30 days and for your keywords (demolition derby, hit to pass, wing night…). These show up as orange "unconfirmed" cards — always tap through to double-check.

Watch keywords

Events matching these get highlighted, and tracked pages are scanned for them. One per line.
Why can't it search Facebook or Instagram?

Facebook shut off public event search for apps in 2018, and Instagram has no event data API at all — there is no legitimate way for any third-party app to search them without you being logged in. That's why racing events "hide" on Facebook pages.

The workaround that works: almost every racing org that posts on Facebook also has a plain website or a page on MyRacePass (search your track name there). Add that link under "My tracked pages" above and Event Scout will watch it for you — no Facebook account needed.
