How to get the free keys

Ticketmaster (concerts, sports, motorsports, comedy):
1. Go to developer.ticketmaster.com and create a free account.
2. It creates an app for you automatically — open "My Apps" and copy the Consumer Key.
3. Paste it above. That's it — the free tier allows 5,000 searches a day.

SeatGeek (includes resale tickets, similar to StubHub):
1. Go to seatgeek.com/account/develop, sign up, register an app.
2. Copy the Client ID and paste it above.

What about Facebook and Instagram?

Facebook shut off public event search for apps in 2018, and Instagram has no event data API at all — no third-party app can search inside them without you being logged in. That's why racing events "hide" on Facebook pages.

What Event Scout does instead: its web sweep specifically searches for publicly indexed facebook.com event pages near your city (those usually open fine without an account), and it automatically finds race tracks near you from OpenStreetMap and scans their websites. Many orgs also post on MyRacePass (search your track name there) — add that link under "Extra tracked pages" and it gets watched too. Private group posts are out of reach for every app, not just this one.
